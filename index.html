About Me

Where creativity
meets precision

I am Menna Hany, a creative professional working at the intersection of technology and visual design. My work is driven by a deep appreciation for structure, detail, and aesthetic balance.

I do not see design as decoration, nor development as code alone — I see both as tools for building meaningful digital experiences. Whether creating a digital interface, a visual identity, or a branded design solution, I focus on producing work that feels sophisticated, clear, and memorable.

My goal is always to create something with value — something that reflects both beauty and intelligence, giving every project a strong, confident presence.
